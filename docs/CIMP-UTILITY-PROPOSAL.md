# CIMP purpose — proposal for review

Status: DRAFT. This does not authorize a mainnet launch, sale, airdrop, staking, or redemption.

## First purpose: learn and test

Use Base Sepolia CIMP as a practice token for connecting a wallet, inspecting a verified contract,
receiving a transfer, and sending tokens. Keep these activities separate from the simulated CIMP market
and the server's 100 pretend USDC portfolio. No conversion exists among those three balances.

## A later community direction

After testing with actual members, consider participation badges for completing lessons or contributing
translations and educational material. Prefer ordinary account badges unless transferring ownership is
necessary. Earning a badge or using the platform should not require purchasing a token.

## Decisions still required

- Who is the intended user, and what problem does a transferable token solve?
- Which features need a blockchain rather than a normal database?
- What benefit can users already access, rather than a promised future benefit?
- If a mainnet token is eventually justified: decide supply, allocations, vesting, treasury authority,
  contract review, operating costs and applicable obligations before a separate launch decision.

Do not treat paper profits as redeemable assets, promise returns, or imply a token creates revenue or liquidity.
The provisional 1,000,000 test CIMP supply is not a mainnet distribution plan.
