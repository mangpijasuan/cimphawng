Your next steps

The website, paper portfolio, and blockchain token are separate systems. Follow these steps in order.

01 · MERGED

Modern workspace

The navy and emerald interface is merged into the repository and published here.

02 · CHECKING SERVICE

Wallet sign-in & persistent paper portfolio

On Home, find Paper trading and press Connect wallet. Review the sign-in message: it is free, sends no transaction, and asks for no token approval. You start with 100 pretend USDC. Swaps use real Base market prices, a simulated 0.3% fee, and no real funds. If the price feed is unavailable or stale, swaps stop.

Use the same wallet to recover its portfolio on another device. Switching wallets requires signing in again. A rejected signature leaves you signed out.

03 · YOUR WALLET APPROVAL REQUIRED

Deploy test CIMP

  1. Open CIMP Testnet Lab and connect your own wallet.
  2. Switch to Base Sepolia, chain 84532, then reconnect if requested.
  3. Obtain Base Sepolia test ETH from a faucet linked in Base’s official documentation.
  4. Read the testnet acknowledgment, then select Deploy 1,000,000 test CIMP.
  5. Check the network and gas in your wallet, then approve. This is a blockchain transaction, unlike the free sign-in message.
  6. After confirmation, keep the contract address and transaction hash. Deploy only once; other testers can load the same contract.

Do not enter or share a seed phrase or private key. Test CIMP has no claimed monetary value. No public deployment address is prefilled because you have not deployed it yet.

04 · TEST BEFORE EXPANDING

Check the full journey

Sign in, make a small paper swap, reload and confirm it persists, then sign out and reconnect. After deploying test CIMP, send a small test transfer to a second test wallet and confirm both balances on Base Sepolia’s explorer.

Server tests cover replayed and expired signatures, account isolation, concurrent spending, reset conflicts, stale prices and invalid inputs. Actual wallet prompts and physical-device behavior still need your review.
