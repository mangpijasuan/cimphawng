Private beta · Educational paper trading

Three ways to explore

  1. Guest demo: explore without a wallet. Demo state stays in your browser.
  2. Saved paper portfolio: sign a free sign-in message from the home page. Practice with 100 pretend USDC, ETH and cbBTC. Stale prices block fills.
  3. Token Lab: use Base Sepolia test tokens. Test tokens have no promised value. Check every wallet prompt and keep recovery phrases private.

Paper trades and the displayed 0.3% simulated fee never move real funds. Scheduled bots and real trading are unavailable on this hosted release.

When something goes wrong

After a timeout, retry the same paper action with the same amount. This tab remembers the request ID and prevents a second fill. Keep the tab open until the result is resolved. For a stale feed, wait and check service status.

For wallet changes, sign in again. Sign-in uses a free message, never a token approval. Deletion and signing out all sessions require a signature from the last 10 minutes.

Report an issue with the time, page and request ID. Never post signatures, cookies, recovery phrases or private keys.

Privacy notice · beta draft

The hosted app stores your public wallet address, paper balances, trade history (including archived trades after reset), sign-in nonces, hashed session tokens and request counters. Sessions expire after 30 days. Rate limits use a hash of the network address supplied by the host. Operational counters and price-feed diagnostics help diagnose failures.

Use Account to export your records or delete your active paper account. Deletion removes its paper records and sessions; it cannot change blockchain activity. Hosting provider backups may retain historical copies for their retention period. Provider retention, production ownership and data licensing must be confirmed before public launch.

Beta terms · draft for review

This is experimental educational software. Paper performance is simulated and does not predict trading results. Prices and availability are not guaranteed. You control your wallet and must inspect any transaction you submit in the Token Lab. Do not treat test tokens as an investment.
